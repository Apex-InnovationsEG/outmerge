What OutMerge does

  1. Lists from wherever they live

    Import Excel or CSV, Outlook contacts or a distribution group. First and last names are kept apart, so {First Name} always reads naturally.

  2. A real editor, in both directions

    Bold, colour, links, lists, tables and pasted images. Switch any message or paragraph to right to left for Arabic.

  3. Sent from you, not a robot

    Every email goes out from your own Outlook account with your signature, and sits in your Sent Items like any other.

  4. Results for every single person

    Sent, waiting in Outbox, failed and why. Resend the rest in one click, or export everything to Excel.

Five checks between you and a wrong email

  1. Preview any recipient exactly as they will see it.
  2. The pre-send check flags empty fields and bad or duplicate addresses.
  3. Send up to five personalised tests to yourself.
  4. Confirm the count and the sending account before anything leaves.
  5. Pause, resume or cancel at any time. An email already handed to Outlook is never sent again.

Questions

Does it work with the new Outlook or Outlook on the web?

No. OutMerge works through the classic Outlook for Windows, 2016 or later, which lets other programs on the PC send email through it. The new Outlook does not.

Where is my data?

On your PC only, encrypted for your Windows account. Your lists, emails and history are never uploaded, and emails leave only through your own Outlook. OutMerge never asks for a password.

Windows warns me when I run it. Is it safe?

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" for apps that are not signed with a commercial certificate. Choose More info, then Run anyway. To be sure the file is the one published here, run Get-FileHash .\OutMerge.exe in PowerShell and compare the result with the SHA-256 above.

Will Outlook ask me to allow each email?

Not on a PC with up-to-date antivirus, which is the usual case. If your organisation's settings make Outlook ask, OutMerge tells you before you send.

How do I know an email was really sent?

OutMerge counts an email as Sent once its copy is in your Outlook Sent Items. Until then it shows it as waiting in your Outbox, and checks again every minute and whenever you press Check Sent Items now. Once Outlook has an email, OutMerge never sends it again.

How do I update?

In OutMerge, open Settings and press Check for updates. If a new version exists, OutMerge shows what changed and downloads it only when you say so. It checks the file's SHA-256 and signature before it installs, and your lists and history stay as they are.
